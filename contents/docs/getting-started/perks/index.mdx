# Perks

Perks are a way to support the development of Gato.

As we know, discord bot are now becoming more and more advanced, and the price for it
drastically increased. No bots nowadays or a few of them are free anymore.

In this scenario, you may ask yourself: **Why would I use Gato instead of another bot?**

**Gato is 100% free**, maintained and improved for great user experience, the bot doesn't need to be purchased. All the essential commands and features
are available for free. You can join our server [here](/https://discord.gg/VjjgZcXh9K) to support us !

We believe in sharing for the community, and giving access to everyone a bot that can be useful.

## Discord Perks

Discord released a feature that allow bot developpers to publish monthly subscription for their bot.
We call this feature **Perks**. Each perks has it own avantages, such as exclusive features and more.

As the bot continue to grow, we don't exclude in the future thats some feature, really advanced one
will be payant, in this scenario, it's you, the community who will support the developpement of Gato,
through monthly subscriptions. All essential features will always remain free, and **old essential features
will never become payant**. If you don't use perks, you will never be limited or restricted in the usage
of the bot.
